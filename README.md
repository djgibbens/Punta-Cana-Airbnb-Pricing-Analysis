# Punta Cana Airbnb Pricing Analysis: Multivariate Modeling
**Technical Toolkit: R | Tidyverse | Multiple Linear Regression | ggplot2**

## Executive Summary
This project expanded upon initial market research to develop a robust multiple regression model for predicting Airbnb prices in Punta Cana. By analyzing the interaction between guest capacity, bedroom count, and bathroom count, I identified the primary structural drivers of rental value.

## Audit & Quantitative Relevance
This analysis demonstrates advanced competency in **Multivariate Statistical Auditing**:
* **Predictive Benchmarking:** Developing "expected price" models to identify market outliers or potential valuation risks.
* **Variable Interaction & Multicollinearity:** Identifying overlapping predictors (e.g., the relationship between guest capacity and bedroom count) to ensure model integrity.
* **Data Cleansing & Normalization:** Transforming disparate listing data into numeric formats suitable for high-level statistical testing.

## Key Findings
* [cite_start]**Structural Drivers:** Property capacity (guests) and structural size (bedrooms/bathrooms) explain approximately 47% of price variation (R² ≈ 0.47).
* **Statistical Insights:** Identified that while all three factors are correlated with price, "guest capacity" often acts as the strongest singular predictor in a combined model.
* [cite_start]**Model Interpretation:** Addressed the "negative bedroom coefficient" as a statistical artifact of multicollinearity, demonstrating professional skepticism toward automated model outputs[cite: 574].
